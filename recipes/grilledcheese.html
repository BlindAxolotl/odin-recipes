Grilled Cheese Recipe

Description

A easy and tasty grilled cheese recipe designed to excite your tastebuds while supplying critical nutrients.

Ingredients

  • Bread 2pc
  • American Cheese 2 slices
  • Butter 1tsp

Steps

  1. Heat up a nonstick pan to medium heat.
  2. Once the pan is heated up, add butter and let it melt.
  3. Add a piece of bread to the pan, add both slices of cheese, and then top with the other piece of bread.
  4. Let the bread toast for about 2 minutes each side.
  5. Once the bread is toasted on either side and the cheese is melted, you are ready to eat!
