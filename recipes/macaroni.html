Macaroni Recipe

Description

A easy and tasty Macaroni recipe designed to excite your tastebuds while supplying critical nutrients.

Ingredients

  • Macaroni Pasta 1lb
  • Macaroni Sauce 16oz

Steps

  1. Cook pasta in boiling water following the package instructions.
  2. While the pasta is cooking, add the jar of sauce to a sauce pan and start heating it over medium heat.
  3. Once the pasta is done, drain the pasta and let it sit until the sauce is done.
  4. Once the sauce is done, add the sauce to the pot with the pasta.
  5. Finallly, serve the Macaroni in a bowl and add parmesean cheese to the top.
