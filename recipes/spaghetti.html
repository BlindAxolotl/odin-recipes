Spaghetti Recipe

Description

A easy and tasty spaghetti recipe designed to excite your tastebuds while supplying critical nutrients.

You can get creative by adding toppings like parmesean and meatballs.

Ingredients

  • Angel Hair Pasta 1lb
  • Spaghetti Sauce 16oz
  • Parmesean Cheese 8oz

Steps

  1. Cook pasta in boiling water following the package instructions.
  2. While the pasta is cooking, add the jar of sauce to a sauce pan and start heating it over medium heat.
  3. While the pasta and sauce are cooking, shred the parmesean cheese.
  4. Once the pasta is done, drain the pasta and let it sit until the sauce is done.
  5. Once the sauce is done, add the sauce to the pot with the pasta.
  6. Finallly, serve the spaghetti in a bowl and add parmesean cheese to the top.
